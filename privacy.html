GoalLens for Jira — Privacy Policy

Developer: Angga Gumelar (independent developer). Contact: adaragumelard@gmail.com. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

GoalLens is a Jira issue panel app built for the Codegeist 2026 hackathon. It shows the Atlassian goals linked to a Jira issue, together with the latest goal update and a deterministic confidence indicator.

What data the app accesses

When you open a Jira issue, the app reads, through Atlassian's own APIs and only with your permission: the issue's linked goals, the latest updates of those goals, and the teams of the goal owners. No other data is accessed.

What data the app stores

Nothing. The app stores no personal data, sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and performs no tracking. Data read from Atlassian is shown only to you inside the Jira issue panel and is not sent anywhere else.

Third parties and AI

The app shares data with no third party and uses no AI or external processing services. All confidence indicators are computed deterministically from the goal's latest update state and age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.
